Go-to-Market Strategy: The SwadesiGo Circle

A capital-efficient, demand-first expansion model powered by 100 hyper-local champions per city, zero physical retail, and community-driven product validation.

Wave 1 — Prove & Optimize

Months 0-12

  • Launch in 5 cities: Pune, Jaipur, Indore, Lucknow, Bhubaneswar
  • Recruit 100 Champions per city (500 total)
  • Validate product-market fit with gig workers and students
  • Achieve ₹50 Cr annualized run-rate revenue
  • Target: 8,000 units sold
Wave 2 — Scale & Network

Months 13-24

  • Expand to 9 Tier-2 cities: Ahmedabad, Coimbatore, Kochi, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Nagpur, Vadodara, Chandigarh, Surat
  • Scale to 1,400 Champions across 14 cities
  • Introduce basic retail micro-hubs in high-demand areas
  • Target: 28,000 cumulative units, ₹180 Cr ARR
Wave 3 — Consolidate & Monetize

Year 3+

  • Expand to 25+ cities nationwide
  • Transition Champions into formal dealer network
  • Launch connected services (battery-as-a-service, insurance)
  • Target: ₹500+ Cr revenue, 80,000+ cumulative units

The "100 Champions" Community Distribution Model

1

Identify & Recruit

Target 100 hyper-local early adopters per city: delivery riders, college influencers, SME owners.

2

Provide Champions with demo units, sales training, and 15-20% commission structure.

3

Champions offer test rides, generate community trust, and provide real-world usage data.

4

Scale & Systemize

Proven Champions transition to formal dealer roles as volume grows and retail micro-hubs open.
